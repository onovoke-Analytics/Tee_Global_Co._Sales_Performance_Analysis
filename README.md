# Tee_Global_Co._Sales_Performance_Analysis
Tee Global Co. Sales Performance Analysis is a Power BI project evaluating revenue, sales volume, customer activity, product performance, geography and time trends. Insights show £4.73M revenue, 554K units sold, strong UK market dominance, higher weekday sales and notable daily/hourly fluctuations, showcasing Power BI, DAX and data modelling skills.

In-Analysis Observations

Strong Overall Revenue Performance: Tee Global Co. generated approximately £4.73M in total revenue from 554K units sold, indicating substantial sales activity during the reporting period.

Strong UK Market Dominance: The United Kingdom generated approximately £4.16M, representing about 87.9% of total revenue, highlighting the UK as the company's primary revenue market and a major source of commercial strength.

High UK Sales Volume: The UK accounted for approximately 482,459 units, representing about 87.1% of total quantity sold, confirming that the country's dominance extends across both revenue and sales volume.

Significant Weekday Performance: Weekdays generated approximately £3.153M (67%) of total revenue compared with £1.581M (33%) on weekends, indicating substantially stronger customer purchasing activity during weekdays.

High Daily Revenue Variation: Daily revenue fluctuated considerably, with the highest visible daily performance reaching approximately £600K. This suggests that specific days or trading events contributed disproportionately to overall revenue.
Strong Hourly Revenue Concentration: Hour 9 generated approximately £605K, representing about 12.8% of total revenue and making it the highest visible hourly revenue period.

Significant Hourly Performance Gap: The highest visible hourly revenue of approximately £605K compared with the lowest of approximately £71K represents an approximately 8.5× difference, highlighting substantial variation in revenue productivity across trading hours.

International Market Opportunity: Spain, Switzerland, USA and Sweden recorded significantly lower revenue than the UK, indicating potential opportunities for market development and geographic diversification.

Product Performance Concentration: Products such as Zinc Willie Winkie Candle Stick, Zinc T-Light Holder, Zinc Wire Letter Tray and Zinc Wire Kitchen Organizer feature prominently in the product analysis, highlighting the importance of understanding their revenue, volume and profitability contribution.

Revenue Concentration Risk: The strong dependence on the UK market creates a potential concentration risk, as any significant decline in UK demand could have a material impact on overall business performance.

Data Reconciliation Opportunity: Some country and product visuals appear to represent subsets of the overall dataset rather than the complete £4.73M revenue base. This highlights the need to validate filters, Top-N selections, DAX measures and aggregation logic before using individual visual totals for executive reporting.

Recommendations

Reduce Geographic Concentration: Tee Global Co. should pursue a measured international expansion strategy while protecting its strong UK market.

Potential target markets should be assessed based on:

•	Market size 

•	Product demand 

•	Customer acquisition cost 

•	Logistics 

•	Shipping costs 

•	Competition 

•	Gross margin 

•	Customer purchasing behaviour 

The objective should be diversification without weakening the UK franchise.
Protect and Grow the UK Market: Given that approximately 88% of revenue comes from the UK, customer retention should remain a major priority.

Management should monitor:

•	Customer retention

•	Repeat purchase rate 

•	Customer lifetime value

•	Average order value 

•	Customer frequency 

•	Customer churn 

•	Top customer contribution 

Investigate the Highest-Revenue Day: The approximately £600K daily revenue peak should be investigated at transaction level.
The analysis should drill down: Date → Country → Customer → Product → Quantity → Hour → Revenue. Identifying the drivers of this peak could reveal strategies that can be replicated.

Optimize High-Performing Hours: The approximately £605K revenue recorded at Hour 9 warrants further investigation. Management should determine whether this pattern is associated with:

•	Customer ordering behaviour 

•	B2B transactions 

•	Marketing activity 

•	Product availability 

•	Specific countries

•	Promotional campaigns 

Resources and marketing campaigns could potentially be aligned with high-performing periods.
Leverage Weekday Demand: Since weekdays generate approximately 67% of revenue, the company should ensure that:

•	Inventory is available during peak weekday periods

•	Sales resources are appropriately allocated 

•	Marketing campaigns target high-performing periods 

•	Customer engagement is intensified during high-demand periods 

Weekend promotions can also be designed to improve the relatively lower 33% weekend contribution.

Conduct Product Profitability Analysis: Product performance should be evaluated using: Revenue + Quantity + Cost + Gross Profit + Gross Margin This will allow management to distinguish between:

•	High-volume products 

•	High-revenue products

•	High-margin products 

•	Low-margin products 

•	Products requiring promotional support 

Conclusion:

The Tee Global Co. Sales Performance Analysis demonstrates a business with strong revenue generation and substantial sales volume, but with significant concentration across geography and time.

The most important finding is the dominance of the United Kingdom, which contributes approximately 87.9% of total revenue and 87.1% of total quantity sold.

The analysis also reveals a strong weekday sales pattern, with weekdays generating approximately 67% of revenue, compared with 33% on weekends.

Daily and hourly analysis further demonstrates that sales performance varies significantly across the reporting period. The highest visible hourly revenue of approximately £605K is particularly noteworthy and warrants further investigation.

From a strategic perspective, the greatest opportunities are:

•	Protect the UK customer base 

•	Reduce geographic concentration through carefully selected international markets 

•	Identify and replicate the drivers of high-revenue days 

•	Optimize high-performing trading hours 

•	Improve weekend performance 

•	Identify high-margin products 

•	Strengthen data quality and dashboard governance 

Overall, the dashboard provides management with a strong foundation for data-driven sales planning, market expansion, customer management and revenue optimization.
